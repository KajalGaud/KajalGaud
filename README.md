## Hi, I'm Kajal 👋
I'm a data analyst (fresher) and a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student, graduating in 2027. I like the moment when a messy spreadsheet turns into a clear answer someone can act on, and that's the work I want to do every day.
I don't have job experience yet, so I built projects that work the way real analysis work does: raw data, cleaning, SQL, a dashboard, and a point of view at the end.

**I'm open to:** junior data analyst roles and freelance projects.

---

## What I can help with
- **Cleaning and preparing messy data** so the numbers can be trusted
- **SQL analysis** (MySQL): joins, CTEs, window functions, segmentation
- **Power BI dashboards** that track the metrics a business cares about
- **Excel dashboards** with PivotTables, PivotCharts and slicers, for teams that live in Excel

**🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis](https://github.com/KajalGaud/customer-segmentation-rfm-analysis-sql-powerbi)**
I segmented 5,878 online-retail customers into six groups by how recently, how often and how much they buy. Champions were about 23% of customers but brought in about 69% of revenue, while nearly half the customer base contributed only about 11%.

**💊 [Pharmacy Sales & Expiry-Risk Analysis](https://github.com/KajalGaud/pharmacy-sales-and-inventory-risk-analysis-sql-powerbi)**
I analyzed 18 months and about 50K pharmacy transactions (about 260M LKR in revenue), then flagged every sale by how close the medicine was to its expiry date, because in a pharmacy that matters.

**👗 [Myntra Fashion Catalog Analysis](https://github.com/KajalGaud/myntra-fashion-catalog-analysis-excel)**
I cleaned and analyzed a large fashion catalog in Excel. Women's products make up about 64% of it. Only about 36% of listings have ratings at all, so I flagged how far the rating insights can be trusted.
